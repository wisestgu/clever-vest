# Product

## What Dollo is

A mobile-first app that lets Nigerian retail investors turn USDT into fractional US stocks and turn returns back into naira. Dollo owns the experience, the education and order routing. Licensed partners hold the money (Cleva) and the securities (a US broker-dealer such as Alpaca).

## Who it's for

Young Nigerian professionals, freelancers and students, mostly on Android phones, often outdoors or on the move. Many already hold USDT on Binance P2P, Bybit or a local exchange to protect savings from naira depreciation. They know the brands (Apple, Tesla, Coca-Cola) but not the vocabulary (market cap, TRC20, dividend yield), and they cannot afford whole shares.

## The job

1. Move dollars in cheaply: USDT on Tron is about $1 and a minute.
2. Decide what to buy without jargon: insider moves in plain English, themed collections, a 60-second company snapshot.
3. Buy with whatever is in their pocket: $1 minimum.
4. Learn enough not to get hurt, and get paid a little for it.
5. Get naira out to their own bank when they need it.

## Principles

- **Plain English over finance-speak.** Every metric carries a sentence saying what it means.
- **Never let a user lose money to the interface.** Network warnings name the exact network an address accepts. Minimums are shown before the user acts. Sandbox addresses say they are sandbox.
- **Show the naira.** Dollar amounts show their naira equivalent wherever a decision is made.
- **Honest about who does what.** Dollo is the interface; partners hold the funds. Simulated data is labelled.

## Platform

A responsive web app (React). It works as a phone-first PWA today and maps cleanly onto Flutter or React Native later. Light theme for daylight use, with a selected dark theme for evening use.

## Status

Hackathon MVP (deliverables due Saturday, Nov 21): a working sandbox demo of the full loop, from USDT deposit through a $5 Tesla purchase to a naira withdrawal.
